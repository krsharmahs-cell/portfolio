Projects

Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

Jun 2026 to Jul 2026

A machine learning pipeline that predicts which telecom customers are likely to leave, early enough for a team to step in and keep them.

I built it end to end on 7,000+ customer records: exploratory data analysis, preprocessing, feature engineering and model training. I evaluated it with ROC-AUC, precision, recall, F1-score and a confusion matrix.

A Streamlit dashboard gives a real-time churn-risk score for each individual customer, with SHAP explanations so it's clear which factors drove the score.
